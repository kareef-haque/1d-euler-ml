### INR Background
* Where Do We Stand with Implicit Neural Representations? A Technical and Performance Survey
    * https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2411.03688

### Activation Function Variation
* [SIREN] Implicit Neural Representations with Periodic Activation Functions
    * https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2006.09661
    * SIREN INR, uses sine as activation function
    * good for capturing signals with a level of periodicity
* [WIRE] WIRE: Wavelet Implicit Neural Representations
    * https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2301.05187
    * WIRE INR, uses complex Gabor wavelet
    * good for signals with localized periodic regions
